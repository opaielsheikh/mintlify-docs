# What Happens After Your Meeting

Learn about the automated actions and notifications that occur when your meeting concludes.

## Automatic Post-Meeting Process

When your meeting ends, the AI agent automatically:

1. **Processes Recording** - Converts raw recording to viewable format
2. **Generates Transcript** - Creates accurate text transcript with speaker labels
3. **Creates Summary** - Produces meeting summary with key points and action items
4. **Sends Notifications** - Distributes summaries based on your settings

<Note>
  Processing time varies based on meeting length. Most meetings are processed within 5-15 minutes.
</Note>

## Email Notifications

### Default Behavior

By default, the AI agent sends email notifications to:

- ✅ Meeting organizer
- ✅ All meeting participants (if enabled)
- ✅ Specified distribution list
- ✅ Workspace members (optional)

### What's Included in the Email

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Summary Email">
    **Standard Summary Email Contains**:
    - Meeting title and date
    - Duration and participants
    - Key discussion points
    - Action items with assignees
    - Decisions made
    - Links to full transcript and recording
    - Next steps
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Detailed Email">
    **Detailed Email Contains**:
    - Everything in summary email
    - Speaker-by-speaker breakdown
    - Timestamps for important moments
    - Questions raised and answered
    - Attachments and shared links
    - Sentiment analysis
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Quick Email">
    **Quick Email Contains**:
    - Brief meeting overview
    - Top 3 action items
    - Direct link to full summary
    - Recording link
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Choosing Not to Send Emails

You can configure meetings without email notifications:

#### For Individual Meetings

When scheduling:
1. Expand **Advanced Options**
2. Under **Post-Meeting Actions**
3. Uncheck **"Send email summary"**
4. Save meeting

#### For All Meetings (Default Setting)

1. **Settings → Notifications**
2. **Post-Meeting Emails** section
3. Set **"Send summary emails"** to **OFF**
4. **Save changes**

<Warning>
  Even with emails disabled, summaries are still available in your dashboard.
</Warning>

## Customizing Email Behavior

### Who Receives Emails

Fine-tune email recipients:

1. **Settings → Notifications → Email Recipients**
2. **Configure options**:
   - ☐ Meeting organizer only
   - ☐ All participants
   - ☐ Participants who attended
   - ☐ Specific email addresses
   - ☐ Workspace members
   - ☐ Custom distribution list

### Email Timing

Control when emails are sent:

```yaml
Delivery Options:
  - Immediate: Right after processing (default)
  - Delayed: 30 minutes after meeting
  - Scheduled: Specific time daily (e.g., 5 PM)
  - Digest: Daily summary of all meetings
```

### Email Templates

Customize email content:

1. **Settings → Templates → Email Templates**
2. **Choose or create template**:
   - Executive Summary
   - Detailed Breakdown
   - Action Items Only
   - Custom Template
3. **Customize content**:
   - Include/exclude sections
   - Adjust formatting
   - Add branding
4. **Save template**

## Notification Preferences

### Per-Meeting Basis

Override default settings for specific meetings:

When scheduling a meeting:
1. **Advanced Options → Notifications**
2. **Select notification preference**:
   - Use default settings
   - Send emails to all
   - Send to organizer only
   - Don't send emails
   - Custom recipients

### Global Preferences

Set default behavior for all meetings:

1. **Settings → Notifications**
2. **Configure**:
   - Email notifications: ON/OFF
   - Default recipients
   - Email template
   - Delivery timing
   - Digest preferences

## What Gets Generated

### Always Created

Regardless of email settings, every meeting generates:

- 📝 **Full Transcript** - Complete verbatim text
- 🎥 **Recording** - Video/audio recording (if enabled)
- 📊 **Summary** - AI-generated meeting summary
- ✅ **Action Items** - Extracted tasks and owners
- 🔑 **Key Points** - Important discussion topics
- 📈 **Analytics** - Meeting insights and metrics

### Optional Generation

Based on your settings:

- 📄 **PDF Report** - Formatted downloadable report
- 📧 **Email Distribution** - Sent to participants
- 💬 **Chat Integration** - Posted to Slack/Teams
- 📋 **Task Creation** - Auto-created in project tools
- 📁 **File Storage** - Archived to cloud storage

## Data Retention

### Storage Duration

<Info>
  Default retention periods:
  - **Recordings**: 90 days
  - **Transcripts**: 1 year
  - **Summaries**: Indefinite
  - **Action Items**: Until completed or manually deleted
  
  Customize in **Settings → Data Management**
</Info>

### Accessing Historical Data

Find past meetings:

1. **Dashboard → Past Meetings**
2. **Use filters**:
   - Date range
   - Participants
   - Keywords
   - Status
3. **Search** by meeting title or topic
4. **Click to view** full details

## Privacy Controls

### Limiting Email Distribution

For sensitive meetings:

1. When scheduling, mark as **"Confidential"**
2. Emails sent **only to organizer**
3. Other participants see **notification without content**
4. Full access requires **dashboard login**

### Restricting Access

Control who can view meeting data:

1. **Meeting → Settings** (gear icon)
2. **Access Control**:
   - Participants only
   - Workspace members
   - Specific individuals
   - Public link (with password)
3. **Save access settings**

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Didn't receive email summary">
    **Check these**:
    - Spam/junk folder
    - Email address is correct in your profile
    - Email notifications are enabled
    - Meeting has finished processing
    - Subscription is active
    
    **Find summary**: Always available in dashboard under Past Meetings
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Email sent to wrong recipients">
    **Review**:
    - Meeting notification settings
    - Default recipient settings
    - Calendar invitation participants
    
    **Resend**: Use "Resend Summary" option in dashboard
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Summary is incomplete">
    **Possible reasons**:
    - Meeting ended abruptly
    - Recording failed mid-meeting
    - Processing error occurred
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Check dashboard for partial data
    - Re-process meeting if option available
    - Contact support with meeting ID
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Want to stop receiving emails">
    **Quick fix**:
    1. Click "Unsubscribe" at bottom of email
    2. Or: Settings → Notifications → Turn off email summaries
    
    You'll still get dashboard notifications
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
