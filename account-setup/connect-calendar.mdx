## Connecting Your Calendar

### Google Calendar Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Go to your account settings and select "Integrations" from the sidebar.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/1.png" alt="Settings Navigation" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Calendar Integration">
    Click on "Calendar Integration" to start the process.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/2.png" alt="Select Calendar Integration" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Click "Connect Google Calendar" and sign in to your Google account.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/3.png" alt="Authorize Access" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Permissions">
    Grant the necessary permissions for the AI agent to access your calendar.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/4.png" alt="Grant Permissions" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Sync">
    Choose which calendars to sync and set your preferences.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/5.png" alt="Configure Sync" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    You should now see your calendar connected successfully.
    <img src="/assets/connect-calendar/6.png" alt="Connection Confirmed" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Microsoft Outlook & Apple Calendar

Follow similar steps for Microsoft Outlook and Apple Calendar by selecting the respective options in the Integrations menu.

## Automated AI Agent Joining

Once your calendar is connected, the AI agent can automatically:

- **Detect Scheduled Meetings**: Scan your calendar for upcoming meetings
- **Auto-Join**: Join meetings based on your preferences
- **Send Reminders**: Notify you before the agent joins
- **Skip Personal Events**: Respect privacy settings and excluded calendars

## Managing Calendar Settings

### Auto-Join Preferences

Configure when the AI agent should automatically join:

```yaml
Auto-Join Rules:
  - All meetings with 3+ participants
  - Meetings with specific keywords in title
  - Only meetings you explicitly mark
  - Custom rules based on attendees
```

### Excluded Calendars

Prevent the agent from accessing certain calendars:
- Personal calendars
- Out-of-office calendars
- Private event calendars

### Sync Frequency

Choose how often to sync with your calendar:
- **Real-time**: Instant updates (recommended)
- **Every 5 minutes**: Balanced approach
- **Every 15 minutes**: Light sync
- **Manual**: Sync on demand

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Calendar not syncing">
    - Verify you've granted all necessary permissions
    - Check your internet connection
    - Try disconnecting and reconnecting your calendar
    - Contact support if issues persist
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Agent not auto-joining meetings">
    - Ensure auto-join is enabled in settings
    - Check that the meeting isn't in an excluded calendar
    - Verify the AI agent has calendar access permissions
    - Review your auto-join rules configuration
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Permission errors">
    - Re-authenticate your calendar connection
    - Ensure your organization allows third-party integrations
    - Check that calendar sharing settings are properly configured
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
