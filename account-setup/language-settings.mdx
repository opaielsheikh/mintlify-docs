# Change Language Settings

Configure the language preferences for your AI meeting summaries and transcriptions.

## Changing Your Language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Language Settings">
    Navigate to your account settings and locate the language preferences section.
    <img src="/assets/change-language/changeLang.png" alt="Change Language Settings" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Language">
    Choose your preferred language from the dropdown menu. The AI agent will use this language for:
    - Meeting transcriptions
    - Summary generation
    - Email notifications
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click "Save" to apply your new language preferences. Changes will take effect on your next meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
