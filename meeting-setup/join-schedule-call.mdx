# Getting the AI Agent to Join Your Calls

There are multiple ways to get the AI meeting assistant to join your video calls - either instantly or scheduled for later.

## How to Add the Agent to a Call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Dashboard">
    Navigate to your AI Meeting Assistant dashboard and locate the meeting controls.
    <img src="/assets/scedule-call/step-1.png" alt="Dashboard Overview" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Meeting Details">
    Enter your meeting link or select a calendar event to add the agent to.
    <img src="/assets/scedule-call/step-2.png" alt="Enter Meeting Details" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Set your preferences for transcription language, recording options, and summary format.
    <img src="/assets/scedule-call/step-3.png" alt="Configure Agent Settings" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm & Join">
    Click to confirm and the agent will join immediately or at the scheduled time.
    <img src="/assets/scedule-call/step-4.png" alt="Confirm and Join" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Joining Options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Instant Join">
    Have the agent join an ongoing call immediately:
    1. Paste your meeting link
    2. Click "Join Now"
    3. The agent joins within seconds
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Schedule for Later">
    Schedule the agent for an upcoming meeting:
    1. Select date and time
    2. Add the meeting link
    3. Configure preferences
    4. The agent joins automatically at the scheduled time
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Calendar Sync">
    If you've connected your calendar:
    1. Enable auto-join in settings
    2. The agent automatically detects your meetings
    3. Joins based on your configured rules
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Verification

Once the agent joins, you'll see:

- ✅ A notification that the AI agent has joined
- ✅ The agent listed as a participant
- ✅ Real-time status in your dashboard

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent not joining the call">
    - Verify the meeting link is correct and not expired
    - Check that the meeting has started
    - Ensure your organization allows bot participants
    - Verify you have meeting credits available
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Agent joined late">
    - Schedule meetings at least 30 minutes in advance
    - Check your calendar sync is active
    - Verify the meeting link was added correctly
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
