# How to Schedule the AI Agent for Meetings

Schedule the AI agent in advance to ensure it joins your important meetings automatically.

## Scheduling Methods

### Method 1: Through Calendar Integration

The easiest way to schedule the agent is through your connected calendar.

#### Automatic Scheduling

If auto-join is enabled:
1. Create a meeting in your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.)
2. Add participants and meeting link
3. The AI agent will automatically detect and join the meeting

#### Manual Scheduling via Calendar

1. **Create a meeting in your calendar**
2. **Add the AI agent as a participant**:
   - Email: `meeting-bot@yourdomain.ai`
3. **Include the video call link in the description**
4. **Save the meeting**

The agent will receive the invitation and join at the scheduled time.

### Method 2: Through Dashboard

Schedule meetings directly from your dashboard:

1. **Navigate to "Meetings" in your dashboard**
2. **Click "Schedule New Meeting"**
3. **Fill in meeting details**:
   - Meeting title
   - Date and time
   - Duration
   - Video call link
   - Participants (optional)
4. **Select AI agent preferences**:
   - Recording options
   - Transcription language
   - Summary template
5. **Click "Schedule"**

### Method 3: Bulk Scheduling

For recurring meetings or multiple meetings:

1. **Go to Settings → Bulk Actions**
2. **Click "Bulk Schedule Agent"**
3. **Choose scheduling method**:
   - Import from calendar
   - Upload CSV file
   - Manual entry
4. **Review and confirm**
5. **Click "Schedule All"**

### Basic Settings

  - Title: [Meeting Name]
  - Date & Time: [Schedule]
  - Duration: [Expected length]

## Recurring Meetings

Set up the agent for recurring meetings:

### Option 1: Calendar Sync

1. **Create recurring meeting in calendar**
2. **Add AI agent as participant**
3. **The agent joins all instances automatically**

### Option 2: Dashboard Recurring Schedule

1. **Schedule meeting through dashboard**
2. **Enable "Recurring Meeting"**
3. **Set recurrence pattern**:
   - Daily
   - Weekly (select days)
   - Monthly
   - Custom pattern
4. **Set end date or number of occurrences**
5. **Save schedule**

## Managing Scheduled Meetings

### View Scheduled Meetings

Access your scheduled meetings:

1. **Dashboard → Upcoming Meetings**
2. View list of all scheduled sessions
3. Filter by date, type, or status

### Edit Scheduled Meeting

Modify meeting details:

1. **Click on scheduled meeting**
2. **Select "Edit"**
3. **Update details**
4. **Save changes**

<Note>
  Changes made less than 15 minutes before the meeting may not take effect.
</Note>

### Cancel Scheduled Meeting

Remove the agent from a scheduled meeting:

1. **Navigate to the meeting in your dashboard**
2. **Click "Cancel Agent Attendance"**
3. **Confirm cancellation**

The meeting will remain in your calendar, but the agent won't join.

## Scheduling Limits

<Info>
  Scheduling limits depend on your subscription plan:
  - **Free Trial**: Up to 5 meetings per month
  - **Basic**: 25 meetings per month
  - **Pro**: 100 meetings per month
  - **Enterprise**: Unlimited
  
  Check your current usage in **Settings → Subscription**
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Agent didn't join scheduled meeting">
    **Possible causes**:
    - Meeting link was invalid or changed
    - Calendar integration was disconnected
    - Subscription limit reached
    - Meeting was too close to scheduling time
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Verify meeting link is correct
    - Check calendar connection status
    - Review subscription limits
    - Schedule at least 30 minutes in advance
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can't find scheduled meetings">
    - Refresh your dashboard
    - Check date filters are correctly set
    - Verify you're logged into the correct account
    - Check if meetings were accidentally deleted
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Recurring meetings not working">
    - Ensure calendar sync is active
    - Check recurrence pattern is correctly set
    - Verify subscription supports recurring meetings
    - Re-save the recurring meeting schedule
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
