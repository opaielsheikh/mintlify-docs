# Quick Start Guide

Get started with AI Meeting Assistant in just a few steps and have your first meeting recorded and summarized.

## Overview

This guide will walk you through:
- Creating your account
- Connecting your calendar
- Scheduling your first AI-assisted meeting
- Reviewing your meeting summary

<Info>
  **Time to complete**: ~10 minutes
  
  **What you'll need**:
  - Email address
  - Access to your calendar (Google, Outlook, etc.)
  - A video call platform account (Zoom, Meet, or Teams)
</Info>

## Step 2: Connect Your Calendar

Connecting your calendar enables automatic meeting detection and AI agent scheduling.

### Google Calendar

1. **Navigate to Settings → Integrations**
2. **Click "Connect Google Calendar"**
3. **Sign in and authorize access**
4. **Choose which calendars to sync**

### Microsoft Outlook

1. **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft Outlook**
2. **Click "Connect Outlook"**
3. **Sign in with Microsoft credentials**
4. **Grant calendar permissions**

<Note>
  Calendar integration is optional but highly recommended for automatic scheduling.
</Note>

<Card title="Full Calendar Setup" icon="calendar" href="/account-setup/connect-calendar">
  See detailed instructions for all calendar platforms
</Card>

## Step 3: Schedule Your First Meeting

You have three options to get the AI agent into your meeting:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Automatic (Recommended)">
    **If you connected your calendar**:
    
    1. Create a meeting in your calendar as usual
    2. Add participants and video call link
    3. The AI agent automatically detects and joins
    4. No additional setup needed!
    
    ✅ Best for: Regular recurring meetings
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Manual Invitation">
    **For any meeting**:
    
    1. Create your meeting in Zoom/Meet/Teams
    2. Add `meeting-bot@yourdomain.ai` as a participant
    3. Send the invitation
    4. The agent joins at the scheduled time
    
    ✅ Best for: One-off meetings
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Via Dashboard">
    **Schedule through the platform**:
    
    1. Dashboard → "Schedule New Meeting"
    2. Enter meeting details (title, time, link)
    3. Configure preferences (recording, template)
    4. Click "Schedule"
    
    ✅ Best for: Full control over settings
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 4: Join Your Meeting

When it's time for your meeting:

1. **Join the video call** as you normally would
2. **The AI agent joins** within 1-2 minutes of meeting start
3. **You'll see a notification** confirming the agent joined
4. **The agent appears** in the participant list as "AI Meeting Assistant"

<Info>
  The AI bot is silent during the meeting - it only listens and records. Participants will be notified that the meeting is being recorded.
</Info>

### What the AI Records

During your meeting, the AI captures:
- 🎥 **Video recording** (optional, can be audio-only)
- 📝 **Real-time transcription** with speaker labels
- 🎯 **Key moments** and important quotes
- ✅ **Action items** mentioned during discussion

## Step 5: Review Your Summary

After the meeting ends (usually within 5-15 minutes):

1. **Receive email notification** with summary
2. **Or access via Dashboard** → "Past Meetings"
3. **Click on your meeting** to view full details
