## Member Roles

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin">
    **Full permissions:**
    - Manage all workspace settings
    - Invite and remove members
    - Assign roles to members
    - Access all meetings
    - Manage billing
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Member">
    **Standard permissions:**
    - Schedule and join meetings
    - Access shared meetings
    - View workspace content
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Viewer">
    **Limited permissions:**
    - View shared meetings only
    - Cannot schedule meetings
    - Read-only access
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Inviting Members

### By Email
1. Go to **Workspace Settings → Members**
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses
4. Select role (Admin, Member, or Viewer)
5. Send invitation

### By Link
1. Generate an invite link
2. Set expiration and usage limits
3. Share the link with your team

## Workspace Settings

Configure your workspace preferences:

- **Workspace Name**: Your team's display name
- **Default Language**: Language for transcriptions
- **Auto-share**: Automatically share meetings with workspace
- **Notifications**: Team notification preferences
