# Cancel Your Subscription

Learn how to cancel, pause, or downgrade your subscription.

## Before You Cancel

<Warning>
  Consider these alternatives before canceling:
  - **Pause subscription**: Keep your data, stop billing temporarily
  - **Downgrade**: Switch to a lower-tier plan
  - **Reduce seats**: Keep the plan but remove team members
</Warning>

### What Happens When You Cancel

- ✅ Access continues until end of billing period
- ✅ All data preserved for 30 days
- ✅ Exports available during grace period
- ❌ No new meetings can be recorded
- ❌ Auto-renewals stopped
- ❌ Team features disabled at period end

## Cancellation Options

### Option 1: Cancel Immediately

Stop billing now and lose access:

1. **Settings → Subscription & Billing**
2. **Click "Cancel Subscription"**
3. **Choose "Cancel Immediately"**
4. **Confirm cancellation**
5. **Optional**: Provide feedback

<Caution>
  Immediate cancellation may forfeit remaining paid time. Consider canceling at period end instead.
</Caution>

### Option 2: Cancel at Period End

Access continues until billing cycle ends:

1. **Settings → Subscription → Cancel**
2. **Select "Cancel at end of billing period"**
3. **Current period**: Ends on [Date]
4. **Confirm cancellation**

You'll receive a reminder 7 days before access ends.

### Option 3: Pause Subscription

Temporarily stop billing:

1. **Settings → Subscription**
2. **Click "Pause Subscription"**
3. **Choose duration**:
   - 1 month
   - 3 months
   - 6 months
   - Indefinite
4. **Confirm pause**

<Info>
  During pause:
  - No charges applied
  - Data preserved
  - Read-only access to existing meetings
  - Cannot record new meetings
</Info>

### Option 4: Downgrade Plan

Switch to a lower tier:

1. **Settings → Subscription**
2. **Click "Change Plan"**
3. **Select lower tier** (Basic, Free, etc.)
4. **Review changes**
5. **Confirm downgrade**

Changes take effect at next billing cycle.

## Step-by-Step Cancellation

### For Individual Plans

1. **Log into your account**
2. **Navigate to Settings** (gear icon)
3. **Select "Subscription & Billing"**
4. **Scroll to "Manage Subscription"**
5. **Click "Cancel Subscription"**
6. **Choose cancellation option**:
   - Cancel now
   - Cancel at period end
   - Pause subscription
7. **Provide reason** (optional but helpful)
8. **Confirm by clicking "Yes, Cancel"**

### For Team/Workspace Plans

<Note>
  Only workspace admins can cancel team subscriptions.
</Note>

1. **Workspace Settings → Billing**
2. **Click "Manage Team Subscription"**
3. **Select "Cancel Subscription"**
4. **Review impact**:
   - Number of team members affected
   - Data that will be archived
   - Features that will be disabled
5. **Notify team members** (automatic)
6. **Confirm cancellation**

### For Enterprise Plans

Enterprise cancellations require coordination:

1. **Contact your account manager**
2. **Or email**: enterprise@yourdomain.ai
3. **Provide**:
   - Organization name
   - Account ID
   - Cancellation date preference
   - Migration needs (if any)

## Data Export Before Cancellation

### Export Your Data

Before canceling, export important content:

1. **Dashboard → Settings → Data Export**
2. **Click "Export All Data"**
3. **Choose format**:
   - Complete archive (ZIP)
   - Meetings only
   - Transcripts only
   - Action items and summaries
4. **Select date range** (optional)
5. **Click "Start Export"**
6. **Download when ready** (email notification sent)

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What's Included">
    - All meeting recordings
    - Full transcripts
    - AI summaries
    - Action items
    - Comments and notes
    - Analytics data
    - User preferences
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Export Formats">
    - Video: MP4
    - Audio: MP3
    - Transcripts: TXT, PDF, DOCX
    - Summaries: PDF, JSON
    - Data: CSV, Excel
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Export Size">
    Typical sizes:
    - 10 meetings: ~5-10 GB
    - 50 meetings: ~25-50 GB
    - 100+ meetings: 50+ GB
    
    Large exports may take several hours
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## After Cancellation

### Grace Period

30-day grace period after cancellation:

- **Read-only access**: View all past meetings
- **Download content**: Export data freely
- **Reactivate**: Easy one-click reactivation
- **Support**: Full customer support available

### Reactivating Your Account

Changed your mind?

1. **Log into account** (during grace period)
2. **Banner appears**: "Your subscription is canceled"
3. **Click "Reactivate Subscription"**
4. **Choose plan**
5. **Confirm reactivation**

Instant access restored, all data intact.

### After Grace Period

After 30 days:

- ❌ Account moves to free tier (if available)
- ❌ Or account deactivated
- ⚠️ Data scheduled for deletion in 60 days
- ✉️ Final export reminder sent

## Refund Policy

### Eligible for Refund

You may be eligible if:
- Canceled within 14 days of initial purchase
- Significant service issues
- Billing error occurred

### Request Refund

1. **Settings → Subscription → Request Refund**
2. **Or contact**: billing@yourdomain.ai
3. **Provide**:
   - Account email
   - Reason for refund
   - Purchase date
4. **Response within 2-3 business days**

<Info>
  Refund policy:
  - Full refund: Within 14 days of first purchase
  - Partial refund: Pro-rated for annual plans
  - No refund: After 30 days, unless service issues
</Info>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    **Yes**. You can cancel anytime, whether you're on monthly or annual billing.
    
    - **Monthly**: Cancel with 1 month notice
    - **Annual**: Cancel with immediate or end-of-term effect
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Will I get charged after canceling?">
    **No**. Once canceled:
    - No future charges
    - Current period is final billing
    - Automatic renewals stopped
    
    Check your cancellation confirmation email for details.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens to my data?">
    **Timeline**:
    - **Immediately**: Read-only access
    - **30 days**: Full access continues
    - **60 days**: Export deadline
    - **90 days**: Permanent deletion
    
    Export your data before the 60-day mark.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
    **Depends on timing**:
    - Within 14 days of first purchase: Full refund
    - Annual plan mid-year: Pro-rated refund possible
    - After 30 days: Case-by-case basis
    
    Contact billing for specific situations.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I reactivate later?">
    **Yes**, within limits:
    - **Within 30 days**: Full reactivation, all data preserved
    - **30-90 days**: Reactivation possible, some data may be archived
    - **After 90 days**: New account required, data deleted
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my team's subscription?">
    **Workspace admins only**:
    1. Go to Workspace Settings
    2. Select Billing & Subscription
    3. Cancel team subscription
    4. Team members automatically notified
    
    Individual team members cannot cancel workspace subscriptions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
