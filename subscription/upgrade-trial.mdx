# Upgrade Your Subscription

Upgrade your plan to unlock more features, additional meeting credits, and advanced capabilities.

## How to Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Subscription Settings">
    Go to your account settings and click on "Subscription" or "Billing" to view your current plan.
    <img src="/assets/upgradeSub/1.png" alt="Subscription Settings" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your New Plan">
    Review the available plans and select the one that best fits your needs. Click "Upgrade" to proceed.
    <img src="/assets/upgradeSub/2.png" alt="Choose Plan" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment & Billing

- **Payment Methods**: Credit card, debit card, or invoice (Enterprise)
- **Billing Cycle**: Monthly or annual (save 20% with annual)
- **Prorated Charges**: Upgrades are prorated based on your billing cycle

<Note>
  Your new plan takes effect immediately after upgrade. Any unused credits from your previous plan will be carried over.
</Note>
