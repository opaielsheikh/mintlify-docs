## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Calendar">
    Link your Google Calendar, Outlook, or other calendar platform to automatically detect scheduled meetings.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="AI Agent Joins Meetings">
    Our AI assistant automatically joins your video calls on Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Real-time Transcription">
    Meetings are transcribed in real-time with speaker identification and timestamps.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="AI-Generated Summaries">
    After each meeting, receive comprehensive summaries with key points, decisions, and action items.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Automated Workflows">
    Send summaries to Slack, create tasks in Asana, or log calls to your CRM automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
